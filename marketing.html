Marketing

The Marketing object represents marketing-related data and activities in ServiceTrade, including campaigns, leads, referral sources, and marketing analytics. This resource allows companies to track the effectiveness of their marketing efforts, manage marketing campaigns, and attribute new business to specific marketing channels or initiatives.

Marketing

The marketing resource is used to manage Marketing data within ServiceTrade.

  • Resource URL: /marketing
  • Available Verbs: GET, POST, PUT, DELETE
  • Authentication Required: yes

Response Codes

Verb Example URL Code Reason
GET /marketing 200
GET /marketing/123 200
GET /marketing/123 404 marketing not found
POST /marketing 200 marketing created
POST /marketing 400 invalid data
PUT /marketing/123 200 marketing updated
PUT /marketing/123 400 invalid data
PUT /marketing/123 404 marketing not found
DELETE /marketing/123 204 marketing deleted
DELETE /marketing/123 404 marketing not found
