Company

The Company object represents a business entity within the system. Companies may be service providers, customers, vendors, partners, or other business relationships. A company can have multiple locations, contacts, and other associated data.

Company

The company resource is used to manage Company data within ServiceTrade.

  • Resource URL: /company
  • Available Verbs: GET, POST, PUT, DELETE
  • Authentication Required: yes

Response Codes

Verb Example URL Code Reason
GET /company 200
GET /company/123 200
GET /company/123 404 company not found
POST /company 200 company created
POST /company 400 invalid data
PUT /company/123 200 company updated
PUT /company/123 400 invalid data
PUT /company/123 404 company not found
DELETE /company/123 204 company deleted
DELETE /company/123 404 company not found

Returned Elements for /company/[companyId]

Name Type Value
id integer company entity ID
uri string resource identifier for the company
name string name of the company
