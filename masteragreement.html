Master Agreement

The Master Agreement object represents a governing agreement between a service provider and a Customer that defines the overall business relationship, terms, and conditions. Master agreements typically serve as umbrella contracts that establish the framework for subsequent Contracts, Quotes, Jobs, and Invoices. They can include pricing structures, service level agreements, term lengths, renewal conditions, and other legal and business requirements.

Master Agreement

The masteragreement resource is used to manage Master Agreement data within ServiceTrade.

  • Resource URL: /masteragreement
  • Available Verbs: GET, POST, PUT, DELETE
  • Authentication Required: yes
